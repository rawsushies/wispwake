# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in the **Wispwake** community a harassment-free, welcoming, and constructive experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our community include:
* Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other contributors and players
* Giving and gracefully accepting constructive design and code feedback
* Focusing on what is best for the game, its accessibility, and the open-source community
* Respecting the creative vision established in the *Wispwake — Game Design & Visual Bible*

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:
* The use of sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention or advances
* Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
* Public or private harassment
* Publishing others' private information without explicit permission

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the repository maintainers via GitHub Issues or repository contact channels. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
